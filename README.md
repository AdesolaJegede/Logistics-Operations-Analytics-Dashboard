# Logistics Operations Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
Analysis of 85,000+ logistics records spanning 3 years 
(2022-2024) using Power BI across 14 interconnected tables 
covering fleet operations, driver performance, route 
profitability, fuel efficiency and safety analysis.

## Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Power Query
- Microsoft Excel

## Dashboard Pages
1. Executive Summary
2. Operational Performance
3. Fleet & Driver Performance
4. Customer, Safety & Predictions

## Key Insights
- Business generated $298.62M in revenue with $197.30M 
  gross profit showing strong financial health
- On-time delivery rate of 55.67% means nearly half of 
  all deliveries arrive late
- Portland is the most profitable route at $44M revenue 
  against only $12M in costs
- Contract customers lead revenue at 37.64% ($112.4M)
- Freightliner trucks are the most expensive to maintain 
  at $1.12M
- 37.65% of all incidents are preventable representing 
  64 avoidable incidents
- Fleet downtime peaks every October reaching 20 hours

## Recommendations
- Implement route optimization technology to improve 
  on-time delivery rate
- Introduce idle time limits and fuel efficiency training 
  for high cost drivers
- Replace high maintenance trucks that cost more to 
  maintain than they generate in revenue
- Introduce quarterly DOT compliance and safety training
- Convert Spot customers to Contract customers for more 
  predictable revenue
- Schedule preventive maintenance in September to prevent 
  October downtime spike

## Future Predictions
- Revenue forecast to exceed $105M annually in 2025
- Maintenance costs will rise as fleet ages without 
  a renewal strategy
- Fuel costs will remain volatile without a proactive 
  purchasing strategy
- Safety incident costs will increase if preventable 
  incidents are not addressed
